01 — About

Field notes

I design and model water supply networks, sewerage systems, stormwater drainage, irrigation networks, and firefighting infrastructure — the systems that move water where a development needs it, and away when it doesn't. My work runs the full design lifecycle: hydrological studies, IDF curve development, rainfall-runoff and discharge estimation, 1D/2D hydraulic modelling, and authority approval submissions through to Issued-for-Construction.

I've delivered this across large-scale rail, road, and urban developments in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Romania, and India — coordinating directly with authorities like DEWA and Dubai Municipality, and developers including Aldar and Emaar.
